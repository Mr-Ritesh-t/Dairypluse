# DairyPulse Commercial Business & Monetization Model

Financial analysis, pricing tiers, unit economics, and operational cash flow projections for DairyPulse.

---

## 1. Monetization Strategy: B2B Subscription SaaS
DairyPulse operates as a pure B2B SaaS platform licensed to regional dairies, milk cooperatives, and route contractors.

### Pricing Tiers (Estimates)

| Tier | Target Scale | Monthly Fee | Included Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starter (Micro Dairy)** | Up to 300 active subscribers | **₹1,499 / month** | Customer mobile portal, manual route ordering, basic WhatsApp billing. |
| **Growth (Commercial Dairy)**| 300 to 1,500 active subscribers | **₹3,999 / month** | Smart route optimizer, rider offline mobile PWA, payment gateway integration, automated WhatsApp broadcast. |
| **Enterprise (Multi-Route Plant)**| 1,500+ active subscribers | **₹8,999 / month** | Multi-chilling plant intake, Fat/SNF quality rate charts, custom domain, priority WhatsApp API volume, dedicated support. |

## 2. Payment Gateway Economics (Razorpay / Cashfree)
- **UPI Transactions (Customer to Dairy Owner):** 0% MDR for P2M transactions up to regulatory thresholds, or flat nominal gateway fee (~₹1.50 per transaction on payment aggregators).
- **Credit Card / Netbanking:** Standard 1.85% – 2.0% MDR absorbed by customer or dairy owner per account configuration.
- **Platform Marketplace Settlement (Roadmap Phase 3):**
  - Direct merchant settlement: In the initial phase, payments route directly to the dairy owner's KYC-verified Razorpay sub-merchant account. DairyPulse does NOT hold funds in intermediary escrow, avoiding regulatory PA-PG licensing exposure.

## 3. Unit Economics & ROI for a Typical Dairy (1,000 Liters/Day)
- **Current Monthly Revenue (1,000 L/day @ ₹65/L):** ~₹19,50,000 / month.
- **Current Unrecovered Losses (Manual Disputes & Late Dues):** 4% = **₹78,000 / month lost**.
- **DairyPulse Growth Subscription Cost:** ₹3,999 / month.
- **Net Monthly Value Created:** **+₹74,001 / month recovered** (Over 18x ROI on software spend).
