# DairyPulse User Stories & Epic Catalog

---

## Epic 1: Household Subscription Flexibility & Cutoff Rules

### US-1.1: Vacation Delivery Pause Before 10:00 PM
- **As a** household milk subscriber,
- **I want to** pause my morning milk deliveries for a specified date range via my mobile portal before 10:00 PM,
- **So that** milk is not left spoiling on my doorstep while I am away, and I am not charged for those days.

### US-1.2: 10:00 PM Delivery Cutoff Lockout
- **As a** dairy owner / dispatch coordinator,
- **I want** the system to reject customer skip or quantity modification requests after 10:00 PM for the next morning's shift,
- **So that** my delivery riders have finalized crate packings and route sheets that cannot be altered mid-transit without management knowledge.

### US-1.3: Extra Milk Request Conversion
- **As a** household subscriber,
- **I want to** request 1 or 2 extra liters of Buffalo Milk for an upcoming festival,
- **So that** I don't have to visit an unorganized milk booth in the morning.

## Epic 2: Delivery Rider Field Operations

### US-2.1: Society & Flat Sequenced Route List
- **As an** early-morning delivery rider,
- **I want to** view my assigned deliveries ordered logically by Society, Wing, and Flat number,
- **So that** I can complete my 100+ morning deliveries quickly before 7:00 AM without walking back and forth.

### US-2.2: Offline Stop Confirmation
- **As a** delivery rider in a high-rise building basement or elevator with zero 4G network,
- **I want to** tap "Delivered" and have the action stored locally on my phone,
- **So that** my workflow is uninterrupted and updates sync automatically once I regain network coverage.

---

## Epic 3: Billing, Dues Collection & Online Payments

### US-3.1: Automated WhatsApp Monthly Bill Reminder
- **As a** dairy owner,
- **I want** one-click broadcast of personalized WhatsApp bill reminders with customer-specific balance dues and UPI payment links,
- **So that** 90% of my monthly collection is completed within the first week of the month without calling customers individually.

### US-3.2: Direct UPI Payment Gateway Checkout
- **As a** customer with outstanding milk dues,
- **I want to** tap "Pay via UPI" and complete payment via Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm,
- **So that** my account balance updates to zero immediately and I receive a verified payment confirmation.

---

## Epic 4: Chilling Plant Intake & Inventory Safeguards

### US-4.1: Dock Intake Quality Verification
- **As a** chilling plant supervisor,
- **I want to** enter received milk liters with tested Fat and SNF percentages,
- **So that** substandard milk is flagged immediately and farmer payout rates are calculated accurately from the rate chart.
