# DairyPulse AI Product Company: Stakeholder Personas

Authentic personas representing our target users in the Maharashtra & Indian regional dairy ecosystem.

---

## 1. Dairy Owner Persona
- **Name:** Rajesh Patil (Age 48)
- **Role:** Founder & Proprietor, *Amrut Sahakari Dairy Farm*, Hadapsar / Baramati, Pune.
- **Scale:** 1,200 daily household pouches, 18 B2B tea/sweet shop accounts, 5 delivery riders.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Cash leakage: Customers skip milk informally via WhatsApp, riders forget to note it down, and owners argue at month-end.
  - Driver management: Riders don't log in early, routes take too long, and fuel/salary disputes arise.
  - Souring losses: Evening milk procurement sitting in chillers without temperature monitoring.
- **Key Requirement:** "I want my money collected without arguments on the 1st of every month, and I want to see how many liters left the plant vs. how many were delivered."

---

## 2. Household Subscriber Persona
- **Name:** Ananya Joshi (Age 34)
- **Role:** Software Professional, Mother of 2, Resident at *Rohan Nilay Society*, Baner, Pune.
- **Subscription:** 1.5 Liters Cow Milk daily (Morning Slot: 6:00 AM – 7:30 AM).
- **Pain Points:**
  - Vacation pauses: Calling the milkman at 9:00 PM to skip milk rarely works; milk still gets delivered and charged.
  - Unclear bills: Getting a handwritten slip on the 3rd with unexplained amounts.
  - Sudden guests: Needing an extra liter of buffalo milk for desserts without having to go to a physical booth.
- **Key Requirement:** "Let me pause deliveries with one click before 10:00 PM, pay my bill via UPI on WhatsApp, and know my milk is fresh and pure."

---

## 3. Delivery Rider Persona
- **Name:** Suresh Shinde (Age 26)
- **Role:** Early-Morning Route Delivery Rider (Route R-04: Baner Road).
- **Work Routine:** 5:00 AM to 7:15 AM, 120 society drop-offs, driving a motorcycle with insulated saddle crates.
- **Pain Points:**
  - Complex software: Cannot type long forms with cold fingers at 5:30 AM in dim apartment lobbies.
  - Spotty internet: Apartment basement elevators and gated society towers have zero 4G network.
  - Missing floor/flat numbers: Wandering through society wings looking for unmarked doorsteps.
- **Key Requirement:** "Show me the exact flat sequence in large text, let me tap 'Delivered' in 1 second, and make sure it works completely offline."

## 4. Dairy Plant Supervisor Persona
- **Name:** Ganesh Jadhav (Age 39)
- **Role:** Chilling Center & Dock Intake Supervisor.
- **Work Routine:** Receives milk cans and tanker trucks from village farmers (5:30 AM and 4:30 PM shifts).
- **Pain Points:**
  - Manual paper register entries for fat/SNF grading.
  - Lack of real-time inventory counts when packing pouch machines start.
- **Key Requirement:** "Fast batch entry: Supplier name, liters, fat percentage, SNF, and automatic rate-chart calculation."
